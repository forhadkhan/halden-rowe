# Halden & Rowe: logo options (Picasso, 2026-10-07)

Forhad asked for a new, distinctive logo. The old one (monoline capitals with an arched H/R doorway, kept in
`previous/`) has been dropped completely. Preview sheet: `logo-options.html` (each option on limewash and on night,
at 120 px wide, at the 14 and 18 px header heights, the monogram from 96 down to 24 px, and the favicon at 16, 32
and 64 px with the 16 px raster enlarged). Every option has `options/<key>/{wordmark,monogram,favicon}.svg`.
They are all generated by `src/build_logos.py` (skia-pathops booleans, no font), and the sheet by `src/build_sheet.py`.

**What every option needed:** one idea tied to the name or to the "Drawn to scale" concept, one colour
(`currentColor`), readable at 16 px and at a 120 px wordmark, and no house-and-roof icon, no typed serif, no
tilted squares.

## A. Swing: the R drawn as a floor-plan door swing
Drawn geometric capitals using an architect's line weights: walls are heavy, and the R's bowl is the light
quarter-arc of a door swing hinged on the stem, with the leaf as its bar. The monogram is HR; the favicon is the
R alone. Weakness: the idea sits in one letter, and the ampersand still needs more drawing work.

## B. Et: the ampersand is the mark (recommended, now live)
"&" began as the Latin *et*. Here the E's middle arm runs on to become the t's crossbar, so the two names are
joined by one shared stroke: a partnership drawn as a ligature. The t's top is cut on the oblique of a
draughtsman's dimension tick. The capitals are high-contrast and drawn, not typed (thick stems, hairline arms),
and sit well with Newsreader. Monogram and favicon: the Et.

## C. Hinge: two leaves on one pin
A hinge (a brass door fitting with interlocking knuckles) stands in for the ampersand and is also the monogram:
two parts that only work together. Geometric lowercase. Weakness: in the wordmark the hinge reads as a broken
glyph at header size, and the lowercase looks the most like a stock geometric sans.

## The pick: B
- **Most ownable idea:** a real-estate mark built from the Et ligature is rare; A's door swing exists in plan
  drawings everywhere.
- **Strongest at 16 px:** the Et favicon stays readable in the enlarged 16 px raster. A's R holds up as well; C
  turns into blocks.
- **Best fit for the brand:** the high-contrast capitals match the editorial serif headings, and the oblique
  t-cut links it to the dimension-line signature.
- **Risk to judge:** a reader may say "Halden Et Rowe". The site always has the name in text, and the SVG titles
  say "Halden & Rowe". If that reading bothers you, the fallback is A.

## QA (logo gate `qa_logo.py --svg`)
- Monogram: one WARN. 20% of the ink is thinner than 1 px at 16 px, which is expected: below 24 px the
  favicon (with heavier hairlines) is used instead.
- Wordmark: WARN `thin-detail` and `parts-change` at the gate's default 96 px minimum width, which is narrower
  than the 120 px minimum in DESIGN.md. Hairlines were raised from 2.2 to 2.6 units so they hold about 0.75 px
  in the 14 px header.

## Status
Placeholder identity for a fictional brand. No trademark search has been run, so nothing is screened or
cleared. Forhad approves the pick; the old files are in `previous/` to roll back to.
